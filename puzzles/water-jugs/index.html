Three jugs

Problem

You are given three jugs: a jug filled with 8 units of water, and two empty jugs of size 5 and 3. Pour the water so that the first and the second jug both contain 4 units, and the third is empty. The jugs have no markings: in one pour you can only pour until the source jug is empty or the destination jug is full.

Work out the sequence of pours with the jugs below. Every pour you make is written down for you. When you are done, generate the solution text at the bottom of the page and paste it into the solution editor of the workbench. Your tutor can help you think through the problem in the workbench chat.
